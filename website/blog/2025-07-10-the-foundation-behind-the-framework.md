*Why Robot Framework works (and keeps getting better)*

**Let’s be blunt:** open source doesn’t magically maintain itself. Someone has to pay for infrastructure, review PRs, fund development, organize the community, and make real decisions. For Robot Framework, that “someone” is the Robot Framework Foundation.

{/* truncate */}

And it works.

But it didn’t always look like this.

### A Quick Flashback: Three Years Ago

Back in 2022, I wrote [this blog post](/blog/robot-framework-foundation) to explain what the Foundation is, how we operate, and why it matters.

Since then?

We’ve grown. We’ve improved. And frankly, we’ve become a textbook example of how open source can be supported — without selling out.

### So, What Is the Robot Framework Foundation?

In legal terms, it’s a non-profit association based in Finland. In practical terms, it’s a structured, democratic, community-driven machine that exists solely to keep Robot Framework strong, stable, and evolving.

- We fund and manage development of Robot Framework core.
- We maintain the infrastructure that makes everything run: [robotframework.org](https://robotframework.org/), Slack, the Newsletter, and more.
- We organize RoboCon, support community education, maintain documentation, and manage [certification programs](https://cert.robotframework.org/).
- We run a real governance model: elected board, transparent processes, community-first decisions.

It’s not flashy. It’s just functional. And it works because of the members.

![Screenshot from robotframework.org frontpage.](/img/blog/1_qADVMgiKaLw-YNAgRYFJmA.png)

### Open Source + Real Governance = Sustainable Development

We’ve all seen open source projects collapse under the weight of success. No plan. No governance. No way to scale.

We took a different path.

Our governance model is based on clarity, transparency, and actual rules. There’s a board of directors, elected by the members. Association meetings define the annual roadmap and budget. Workgroups take responsibility for key areas — from conference planning to core development. Anyone in the community can join and contribute.

It’s not perfect. But it’s consistent. It’s accountable. And it’s always improving.

### Why the Foundation Model Matters

Without the Foundation, there would be no RoboCon. No certification program. No funding for releases. No roadmap. No full-time maintenance. No Slack community with over 33,000 members.

The Foundation exists so Robot Framework isn’t just another abandoned GitHub project.

It keeps the tool:

- **Free and open**
- **Actively developed**
- **Technically stable**
- **Community-driven**

If your company or organization uses Robot Framework, the Foundation is already working for you — whether you realize it or not.

### Why You (Yes, You) Should Join

Our members — companies, organizations, and foundations — are committed to ensuring Robot Framework remains strong and free.

**Joining means:**

### Final Word: Real Structure, Real Impact

The Foundation isn’t a feel-good club. It’s a working system that keeps one of the world’s best test automation tools — and its ecosystem — alive and moving forward. It’s legal, structured, documented, and governed — without being stiff or bureaucratic.

Think of it like this:

> *A lighthouse isn’t glamorous. But without one, things crash.*

Robot Framework keeps working because the Foundation keeps showing up.
