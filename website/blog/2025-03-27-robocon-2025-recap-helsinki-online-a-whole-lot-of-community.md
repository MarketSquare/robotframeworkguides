RoboCon 2025 is a wrap. Two formats — in-person in snowy Helsinki and online a few weeks later via Gather.town — and both delivered. Different energy in each, but the same feeling throughout: this community keeps showing up, sharing, and pushing things forward.

{/* truncate */}

Let’s break it down.

The theme colors were shown in Bio Rex. Credits Jani Haapala.

### **📍 Quick Facts**

#### In-Person (Helsinki — Bio Rex)

🗓️ **Feb 11–15, 2025**

- **Tue:** Community Day (unconference-style)
- **Wed:** Hands-on Workshops (Gold ticket)
- **Thu–Fri:** Conference Days
- **Evenings:** Optional Finnish-German dinner (Thu), afterparty (Fri)
- **Sat:** Optional Suomenlinna trip

#### Online (Gather.town)

🗓️ **March 4–7, 2025**

- **March 4:** Tutorials
- **March 5–6:** Talks + Q&A
- **March 7:** Community Day
- All in a virtual world where avatars roam, side chats happen in 2D bars, and yes — there’s a sauna

### 🎤 Helsinki Highlights

Being back at Bio Rex just worked. That classic venue delivers — great auditorium for talks, and the side bar area gave space for quieter convos.

The Finnish theme added flavor:

- Our team, team Robot Framework, repping blue-and-white hockey shirts
- Mölkky on the snowy terrace
- Tabletop hockey and the Robot Framework Snake game
- Finnish food at the conference

Gamification was well-received. Sponsor booths got solid traffic, and the Elevator Pitch competition turned out to be a good mix of pressure and fun.

![A picture of the conference gamification badge with a perfect game — all stickers collected. The gamification added a lot of interactivity to the conference.](/img/blog/1_BZdIPbO4L4cGwEYYuG7atw.jpeg)

The conference gamification badge with a perfect game — all stickers collected. The gamification added a lot of interactivity to the conference. Credits Lisa Böttinger.

A personal favorite: the Finnish-German dinner. It started with *Helan Går* and somehow ended in karaoke with a room full of test automation folks. A night to remember.

The talks themselves hit home — average rating: **4.53 / 5**. A strong sign that the content landed well.

The vibe? Friendly, focused, and grounded.  
**NPS score: 65%** — a clear signal that people left with good energy.

### 🌐 Online Conference Takeaways

The online event had a different kind of flow — more flexible, yet interactive, and very much alive.

**Joe Colantonio** hosted again and kept the pace right. Talks and Q&A were sharp throughout, with an average **talk rating of 4.3 / 5**.

**Gather.town** continues to shine:

- Speaker Corners gave direct access to speakers
- Casual bar/lounge chats felt natural
- Thursday night games and hangouts brought real connection

**Community Day** stood out — no real agenda, just the community building it together. People shared, coded, and collaborated across time zones.

And the feedback matched the vibe:  
**Online NPS: 69%** — excellent for a virtual event.

![A picture from stage with people who have helped to test the RFCP certificate.](/img/blog/1_CyTNwBpN-pFQgsNHU7qypQ.png)

During the online conference, we officially launched the [Robot Framework Certified Professional](https://cert.robotframework.org/) (RFCP) certificate. At the in-person event, we also awarded paper certificates — hand-signed — to some of the people who had already helped test the exam. Credits Jan Lewandowski.

### 🛠️ Tutorials, Workshops & Extras

- **Tutorial Day (March 4)** kicked things off with practical, hands-on sessions online. Great learning you can revisit on our [YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@RobotFrameworkChannel/streams)
- **Workshops (Feb 12 in Helsinki)** gave Gold ticket holders a chance to dive deep with experts. Superior sessions delivered by awesome trainers
- **Suomenlinna trip** on Saturday gave everyone a scenic, low-key end to a high-energy week
